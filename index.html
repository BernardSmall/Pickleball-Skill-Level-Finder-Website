ABOUT PICKLERATE

A rating should explain your game.

PickleRate estimates level from multiple pieces of match-based evidence instead of relying on one result, one highlight or one vague self-rating.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Consistency matters more than isolated brilliance

We do not believe one ATP, one tournament win or one exceptional session defines a player. The assessment focuses on repeatable execution, sound decisions and how reliably those abilities transfer into competitive play.

CONFIDENCE

How well does the evidence agree?

Confidence reflects answer completeness, agreement among related skills and the amount of competitive evidence available. Limited tournament experience should lower certainty—not automatically lower your technical rating.

WHY MULTIPLE QUESTIONS?

Important skills need more than one observation

A third-shot profile can include overall drop success, forehand and backhand execution, adjustment to difficult returns and fifth-shot control. Agreement across these observations creates a stronger estimate than any single question.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions players may have

Can I trust my rating?

Use it as an evidence-based estimate and development guide, not an official replacement for match-based systems or a qualified coach.

Why does confidence matter?

Confidence indicates how complete and internally consistent the evidence is. A similar rating can be supported strongly or weakly.

Why are there 43 questions?

Important skills need several observations. This reduces the influence of one misunderstood question or unusually good session.

Why is DUPR not used directly?

DUPR can be entered as calibration information. PickleRate is designed to explain skills and development areas rather than reproduce a match-rating system.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. Newer players should treat low-confidence results as a baseline and reassess after gaining more match experience.

How often should I reassess?

Reassess after a meaningful block of practice and match play—typically every two to four weeks rather than after every session.
