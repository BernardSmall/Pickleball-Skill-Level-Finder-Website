ABOUT PICKLERATE

A rating should explain your game.

PickleRate estimates level from multiple pieces of match-based evidence instead of relying on one result, one highlight or one vague self-rating.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Consistency matters more than isolated brilliance

We do not believe one ATP, one tournament win or one exceptional session defines a player. The assessment focuses on repeatable execution, sound decisions and how reliably those abilities transfer into competitive play.

CONFIDENCE

How well does the evidence agree?

Confidence reflects answer completeness, agreement among related skills and the amount of competitive evidence available. Limited tournament experience should lower certainty—not automatically lower your technical rating.

WHY MULTIPLE QUESTIONS?

Important skills need more than one observation

A third-shot profile can include overall drop success, forehand and backhand execution, adjustment to difficult returns and fifth-shot control. Agreement across these observations creates a stronger estimate than any single question.
