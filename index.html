      How PickleRate works

      About the assessment

      PickleRate is designed to reward repeatable match performance rather than confidence, knowledge of advanced shots or isolated practice success.

      Evidence-based questions

      Questions focus on measurable outcomes such as successful drops, deep returns, controlled dinks, effective resets and competitive rally performance.

      Weighted categories

      Every question now has importance, difficulty and reliability metadata. Core skills carry more evidence than specialist shots, while difficult skills provide only controlled extra credit when the foundations support them.

      Estimated range

      The website provides a likely range because no self-assessment can identify an exact competitive rating with perfect accuracy.

      Saved progress

      Completed assessments are stored only in your browser using local storage. No account or server is required for this version.
